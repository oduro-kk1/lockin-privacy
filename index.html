LockIn Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

LockIn is a focus and study app published by Nana Obenefour Oduro Kwakwa ("I", "me"). This page explains what the app collects, why, and how you can delete it. Contact: obenefour2120@gmail.com

What stays on your phone

Your PDF library, your reading position, your study sets (flashcards and quizzes), your settings and your focus history are stored on your device. Your PDFs are never uploaded in full.

What is collected when you sign in

  • Google account details (your email and Google ID) so you can sign in. I do not receive your Google password.
  • Display name you choose, shown to your friends.
  • Friends: friend requests and your friends list.
  • Finished focus sessions: mode, minutes, XP and the date, used for the friends-only leaderboard and your streak.
  • Reports: if you report a display name, I record who reported whom.

This data is stored with Supabase (hosted in the EU, Ireland). Only your friends can see your display name and XP. There is no public leaderboard.

AI study tools

When you tap the AI button, the text of the pages you choose is sent through my server to Google's Gemini API to create flashcards and quiz questions. You are asked to agree before this happens. The text is not stored on my server. I keep only a count of how many requests you made each day, to apply daily limits. LockIn currently uses Gemini's free tier. Under Google's terms for free-tier use, Google may use submitted content to improve its products, so do not send documents you consider confidential.

App blocking (Deep Focus)

Deep Focus uses Android's Accessibility Service to notice which app is in front, so LockIn can bring you back when a blocked app (such as Instagram, TikTok or YouTube) opens. It reads only the app's package name. It does not read, record or send anything on your screen, and nothing about it leaves your phone. The service is optional and you turn it on yourself in Android settings.

Notifications

Reminders and focus alerts are created on your device. No notification data is sent to me.

No ads, no selling

LockIn has no ads or trackers, and I do not sell or share your data, other than with the services named above (Supabase and Google Gemini) to run the app.

Children

LockIn may be used by people under 18. Social features are friends-only: there is no public profile and no chat. Display names can be reported.

Deleting your data

In the app, open Account and choose Delete account. This permanently deletes your profile, friends, finished sessions, reports and AI-usage counters from my server. Data on your phone is removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. You can also email me to ask for deletion.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change too.
